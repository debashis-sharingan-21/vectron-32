◆ HARDWARE ABSTRACTION POLICY:

              
Strapping pins (0, 2, 12, 15) must remain in known states during power-up. SPI flash pins (6-11) are physically wired to the 4MB NOR die and are strictly forbidden from manual GPIO multiplexing. All active relay channels feature initial active-low state suppression to eliminate boot relay chatter.
